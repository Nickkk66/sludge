Get better voices

macOS ships with old, robotic voices and keeps the good ones behind a download. Sludge can only use what your Mac has installed — but the natural-sounding ones are free and take a minute to add.

  1. Open System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content.
  2. Click the ⓘ next to System Voice, then Manage Voices…
  3. Under English, download any voice marked Premium — these are the natural ones. Good picks:
    Ava · American, female Tom · American, male Serena · British, female Daniel · British, male
  4. Come back and hit Recheck — they'll appear in the voice list.

Premium voices are a few hundred MB each and run entirely offline once downloaded.
